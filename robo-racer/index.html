Your mission

Program first. Then watch it run.

  1. Observe. Find the robot, beacon, walls, and hazards. The robot moves relative to the direction it faces.
  2. Plan. Choose five cards. Their order is your algorithm.
  3. Predict. Entering a belt ends the current card and carries the robot off its exit. An active wormhole transports the robot to the Warp target when it is available; otherwise it chooses a random empty square. Wormholes toggle with the lasers after each round, while Warp availability is randomized. Any element on an exit square resolves before the next unplayed card runs.
  4. Test and debug. In Easy mode, touching the goal wins immediately. In Hard mode, the robot must occupy the goal after all five cards finish.

Keyboard tip: Tab to a card and press Enter. Select a programmed card to return it to your hand.
