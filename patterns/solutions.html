Guided lesson · Training Line

Rule: shape and color alternate while direction rotates clockwise.

orange circle ↑ · blue square → · orange circle ↓ · blue square ← · orange circle ↑
TaskSolutionReasoning
PredictionOne orange circle pointing upOdd positions share the orange circle; direction completes a four-step rotation.
Earliest supported call4 cluesFour clues establish both the alternating pair and a complete direction cycle.
Common partial rule“The color alternates.”True, but it ignores shape and direction.

Starter · Production Forecast

Rule: orange circles and blue squares repeat ABAB.

orange circle · blue square · orange circle · blue square · [orange circle]
AnswerCommon interpretationUseful response
Orange circle“Only the color changes.”Ask whether the shape also stays the same or changes.
Rule: products alternate“The next one just looks like a circle.”Ask the student to identify at least two repeated pairs as evidence.
  • What stays the same between positions 1 and 3?
  • How could you describe this pattern without using the words orange, blue, circle, or square?

Intermediate · Call the Line

Rule: color alternates purple/green while the triangle rotates clockwise.

purple triangle ↑ · green triangle → · purple triangle ↓ · green triangle ← · [purple triangle ↑]
AnswerCommon interpretationUseful response
Purple triangle pointing up, amount 1“Only the colors alternate.”That explains one feature. What happens to the direction every step?
Earliest supported call: 4 cluesCalling after 2 cluesTwo clues allow many possible rules; request another clue or two for stronger evidence.
  • Which hypothesis becomes less likely after the fourth product?
  • Is calling early always better? What else must an early call do?

Challenge · Quality Control

Rule: odd positions are one yellow diamond; even positions are two blue circles. Direction rotates clockwise.

yellow diamond ↑ · 2 blue circles → · yellow diamond ↓ · 2 blue circles ← · [yellow diamond ↑] · 2 blue circles →
TaskSolutionCommon interpretation
Defective productPosition 5The supplied yellow circle has the right color, amount, and direction but the wrong shape.
ReplacementOne yellow diamond pointing up“Color and amount are enough.” This ignores the alternating shape track.
Complete ruleThree alternating attributes plus clockwise directionStudents may recognize only one or two of the simultaneous changes.
  • Which product features can be examined as separate tracks?
  • How would the same rule look if the factory produced animals or numbers instead?
  • What should positions 7 and 8 contain?
